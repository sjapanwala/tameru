One tap for coffee. A photo for groceries.

  • Matched to your card. The last four digits on the receipt pick the right account.
  • Categories that learn. Change one once and Tameru remembers it for that store.
  • Split it. One receipt can count toward groceries and household at once.

Get the app

On your Home Screen in under a minute.

Tameru is a web app you install from the browser. No app store, nothing to sign up for. Once it’s on your Home Screen it opens full screen like any other app.

  1. Open Tameru in Safari

    On iPhone, use Safari. On Android, Chrome works.

  2. Add it to your Home Screen

    Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, tap Install.

  3. Answer a few questions

    Your pay, your bills, what you’re saving for. Skip anything you’re unsure of. You get your first plan and your number right away.
