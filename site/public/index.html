    Questions

    Does it connect to my bank?

    No. You enter your balances once, then log spending with quick adds, a receipt photo or by hand. Nothing ever asks for a bank password.

    Where is my data stored?

    Only on your phone, in the app’s own storage. There are no Tameru servers holding your data and no account to sign in to.

    Is it on the App Store?

    Tameru is a web app you add to your Home Screen from the browser. Once it’s there it opens full screen and works offline, like any other app.

    What happens if I lose my phone?

    Your data lives on the device, so make a backup from More → Settings whenever you like. A backup file restores everything on a new phone.

    How is safe to spend worked out?

    Spendable balances, plus pay still coming before the period ends, minus bills, goal contributions, money still planned in categories and your buffer. Tap the number in the app to see your own breakdown.
