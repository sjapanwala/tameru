What Tameru is

Tameru is a personal budgeting app. It takes the balances, bills, income, plans and goals you enter and estimates how much is safe to spend. It runs in your browser and can be added to your Home Screen. There’s no charge and no account.

Not financial advice

Tameru is a tool for organising your own numbers. Nothing in the app or on the site is financial, investment, tax, legal or accounting advice. Figures like “safe to spend”, forecasts, interest estimates and plan suggestions are estimates based on what you enter and on assumptions the app makes, such as counting varying pay at its low end. They can be wrong or out of date.

Check important amounts against your bank and card statements, and make your own financial decisions. If you need advice, talk to a qualified professional.
